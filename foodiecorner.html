Our Recipes

Homemade Pizza 🍕

A delicious pizza with cheese and fresh vegetables.

Ingredients:

  • Pizza base
  • Cheese
  • Tomato sauce
  • Fresh vegetables

Instructions:

  1. Spread tomato sauce on the pizza base.
  2. Add fresh vegetables and cheese.
  3. Bake until golden and crispy.

Chocolate Cake 🍰

A sweet and tasty chocolate dessert.

Ingredients:

  • Flour
  • Sugar
  • Cocoa powder
  • Milk

Instructions:

  1. Mix all ingredients into a smooth batter.
  2. Pour the batter into a cake tin.
  3. Bake until fully cooked.

Cooking Tips

  • Always use fresh ingredients.
  • Keep your kitchen clean.
  • Follow the recipe instructions carefully.
  • Wash your hands before cooking.
